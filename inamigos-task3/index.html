01 / WHY WE EXIST

Small actions become
lasting systems.

InAmigos Foundation brings people, resources and local action together around needs that are easy to see — but harder to solve alone.

Founded in 2020, the Section 8 non-profit works across India through focused initiatives in education, women’s empowerment, social welfare, animal care, environmental action and employability.
